about=RockMorph turns QGIS into a complete geomorphology workstation, from the DEM to the publication figure. Terrain: slope, curvatures, yearly insolation, Swiss and multidirectional shading, openness, sky-view factor, local dominance, multi-scale topographic position, valley bottoms, landform classes and geomorphons. Hydrology: sink filling, stream burning, Strahler/Horton/Shreve networks, HAND, MFD and D-infinity accumulation, watershed subdivision. Rivers: longitudinal profiles, SL index, chi-plots with knickpoints, ksn per segment and over the whole network, best concavity, normalised channel profiles. Tectonic geomorphology: 60 published morphometric indices with their classifications and references, mountain-front sinuosity, the relative active tectonics index Iat (El Hamdouni et al., 2008), AHP weighting with sensitivity analysis, composite indices, PCA, clustering and outlier detection. Profiles: topographic, serial cross-sections and swath profiles linked to the map, in 2D and 3D. Structural geology: rose diagrams, three-point dip with predicted outcrop traces, geological map digitizer. 3D: realistic terrain with cast shadows, draped maps with their QGIS style and a hover probe linked to the map. Every result is a QGIS layer or an interactive chart exportable to PNG, SVG, CSV and GeoPackage, and every method cites its source. Charts use Qt WebEngine (Plotly, three.js); GRASS, scikit-learn and umap-learn are optional.
