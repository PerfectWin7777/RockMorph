about=RockMorph is a geomorphology toolkit for QGIS. It computes, classifies and charts 60 published morphometric and active-tectonics indices for your own basins (basin shape, relief and hypsometry, Horton-Strahler network, channel profile SL, ksn and chi, asymmetry factor, transverse symmetry, valley floor ratio, TWI/SPI/STI), combines them into the relative active tectonics index Iat (El Hamdouni et al., 2008), AHP and composite indices, and explores them with PCA, clustering and outlier detection. It also provides fluvial profile analysis (chi-plots, knickpoints, ksn), mountain-front sinuosity, topographic and swath profiles, rose diagrams, a three-point dip tool with predicted outcrop traces, hypsometric curves, terrain derivatives (curvatures, insolation, geomorphons, landforms, valley bottoms), flow routing (HAND, MFD/D-infinity, stream burning, network ksn), watershed subdivision and a 3D explorer with draped maps. Charts use Qt WebEngine and Plotly; optional methods use scikit-learn.
