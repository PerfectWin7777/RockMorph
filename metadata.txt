about=RockMorph is a geomorphology toolkit for QGIS. It computes, classifies and charts 60 published morphometric and active-tectonics indices for your own basins (basin shape, relief and hypsometry, Horton-Strahler network, channel profile SL, ksn and chi, asymmetry factor, transverse symmetry, valley floor ratio, TWI/SPI/STI), combines them into the relative active tectonics index Iat (El Hamdouni et al., 2008), AHP and composite indices, and explores them with PCA, clustering and outlier detection. It also provides fluvial profile analysis (chi-plots, knickpoints, ksn), mountain-front sinuosity, swath profiles, rose diagrams, hypsometric curves, terrain derivatives, flow routing, watershed subdivision and a 3D explorer. Charts use Qt WebEngine and Plotly; optional methods use scikit-learn.
